Our team

How we play

3-4-1: three centre backs, wingers wide, two in the middle and one striker.
Watch the 3-4-1 shape change with and without the ball

In the 3-4-1 we're a structured team. Everyone knows her position and mostly holds it, so our shape stays solid with and without the ball. The back three stay connected, the wingers keep the width, the two centre mids stay central and the striker leads the line.

If someone leaves her position: the nearest centre mid drops into the gap, so the back line stays whole. Get back into your position as soon as you can.

2-4-2: two centre backs, four across the middle with the wing backs wide, then the 10 and the 9.
Watch the 2-4-2 shape change with and without the ball

In the 2-4-2 we're a mix. At the back we're structured: the two centre backs hold their positions and stay connected. From midfield forward we're fluid: the wing backs, centre mids, the 10 and the 9 read the game, swap and make runs into space.

If someone leaves her position: the nearest centre mid drops into the gap, so the back line stays whole. Get back into your position as soon as you can.

If you remember nothing else

Three team rules

The three things every player remembers, whatever the formation.

Rule 1Talk

Call for the ball, call "man on", help each other out. A quiet team is easy to beat.

Rule 2Work back

Lose it, win it back. Everyone gets goal-side straight away. Never stand and watch.

Rule 3Pass, then move

Never stand still after a pass. Give your teammate another option.
