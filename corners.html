In possession

Attacking corners

Routine 1Into the box

Cross it into the six-yard box for our runners at the near post, the centre and the far post.

Routine 2Low to the edge

Pass it along the ground to a player waiting on the edge of the box to shoot.

The takerOn rotation

Designated players take turns, and they know who they are. In-swinging or out-swinging: whatever suits the taker. There's no signal, so the taker picks the routine and everyone reads it.

Gold 1: the cross. Gold 2: the low one. White: runs.

Who goes where

  • Taker: a centre mid, on rotation.
  • In front of their keeper: the striker.
  • Near post: the left winger, running in as the ball is struck.
  • Centre: the other centre mid.
  • Far post: the right winger.
  • Edge of the box: two centre backs. One arrives late for the low one, the other waits just outside the box.
  • Stays back: the middle centre back, on halfway.
  • Taker: a centre mid, on rotation.
  • In front of their keeper: the 9.
  • Near post: the left wing back, running in as the ball is struck.
  • Centre: the 10 and the other centre mid.
  • Far post: the right wing back.
  • Edge of the box: a centre back, who arrives late for the low one.
  • Stays back: the other centre back.

Counter-attack: one centre back always stays back.

Out of possession

Defending corners

Shaded circles: each player's zone. The ball is on our left corner flag.

We defend corners zonally. Guard your zone, not a player. If the ball comes into your zone, it's yours: attack it and clear it.

Who goes where

  • Posts: a centre back on each post.
  • Keeper: in the middle of her goal.
  • Six-yard box: a centre mid, ready to step to the near post. The middle centre back guards the space in front of her.
  • Near side: a winger guards the near-post zone and steps out to stop the short corner.
  • Far side: the other winger.
  • Edge of the box: the other centre mid, for clearances and their player waiting on the edge.
  • Outlet: the striker stays up on halfway, far side, ready for the counter-attack.
