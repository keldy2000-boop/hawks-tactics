This block

What we're working on

This block's focus is playing forward: win the ball high, move so the next pass is on, and finish it. Every Thursday works on a few of The Ten Rules, and on match day the coaches shout the short version of each rule.

The Ten Rules Each rule has a short clip, what to do and what not to do. Watch the clips before Thursday. Open The Ten Rules →

What you should be trying to improve

STStrikersRules 1, 5, 6 · the 9 and 10 in 2-4-2
  • Curve your press to win it high.
  • Hide on the blind side, then go.
  • Start deep, arrive late.
CMCentre midsRules 2, 3, 4, 8
  • Scan before the ball arrives and open your body.
  • Pass, then move.
  • Busy one side? Switch it.
WWide playersRules 6, 7, 8 · wingers and wing backs
  • Far side: stay wide and ready for the switch.
  • Overlap on the outside.
  • Start deep, arrive late.
CBCentre backsRules 2, 4
  • Push up behind the press.
  • Pass to space, not feet.
GKKeeperRule 10
  • Step out and close the angle.
  • Then get us going forward quickly.
ALLEveryoneRules 3, 9
  • Pass, then move. Never stand and watch.
  • Chest over the ball when you shoot.

Work on it at home

  • Look before it arrives (Rule 2): the scanning game in Practise at home. Call the number before every touch.
  • Wall passes (Rules 2 and 3): open your body as the ball comes back, and take your first touch forward.
  • Watch your position (Rules 5 and 6): watch a match and spot when a striker hides on the blind side, or starts deep and arrives late.
  • Strike practice (Rule 9): plant your foot beside the ball, chest over it, laces through the middle. Ten shots at a wall or a goal, both feet.

Ten minutes in the garden

Practise at home Draft

Little and often beats a long session once in a while. Pick one, do it for ten minutes, and try to beat your score next time.

First touchWall passes

Pass against a wall and control it before passing again. Use both feet: left, right, left. Count how many in a row without losing it.

Ball controlKeepy-ups

Feet only, then add thighs. Write down your best and try to beat it each week.

DribblingCone weave

Set out five markers (shoes or bottles work) and weave through them using the inside and outside of both feet. Then do it faster.

ScanningLook before it arrives

With a family member passing, glance over your shoulder before every touch. They hold up fingers and you call the number before you receive.

Weaker footWrong-foot week

Use only your weaker foot for every touch. It feels awkward at first. That's how you know it's working.

WatchingWatch your position

Watch a match and follow one player in your position. Where does she stand when her team has the ball, and where does she go when they lose it?
