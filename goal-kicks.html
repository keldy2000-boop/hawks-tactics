In possession

Our goal kicks

Gold 1: first choice. Blue 2: second choice. Then down the line for the runner (white).

Short or long? A mix. The coaches decide on the day, depending on the other team and the score.

The keeper's passing order

  1. Wide to the wingerwing back on the touchline.
  2. Centre mid showing in the middle, if the wide player is marked.
  3. Long if neither is on.

Our pattern

GK → wide → down the line. The wingerwing back takes it and plays it first time down the line for a runner: the nearest centre midthe 10, who starts deep and arrives late (Rule 6).

If they press with two forwards, or man-mark everyone: go long.

Out of possession

Their goal kicks

Watch our press on the board
Our starting shape against their 3-4-1. Gold: the trigger pass. Blue: our press.

Press high or drop off? It depends on them. If they play it short, we press. If they usually go long, we drop off into our shape. The coaches call it.

Who presses

The striker leads the press, with the nearest winger squeezing up alongside her. Curve your run to cut off the pass (Rule 1).

The 9 closes down their middle centre back and curves her run so the only pass is out wide (Rule 1). When it goes wide, she curves across to block the pass back to the middle. The 10 stays on her side and presses the wide centre back. The 9 and 10 don't cross.

Triggers: everyone goes together when

  • their keeper passes short to a centre back
  • their player takes a bad touch
  • their player receives facing her own goal

If they…

  • Go long: we don't encourage heading. The centre backs and centre mids deal with it.
  • Switch it across the back: drop into our shape.
  • Throw or roll it out: the nearest player presses whoever receives it, and the rest squeeze up.
