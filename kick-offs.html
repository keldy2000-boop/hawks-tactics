In possession

Our kick-off

Watch our kick-off move on the board
Gold 1: the tap back. Gold 2: the high ball down our left. White: runs.

The routine

  1. The striker taps it back to the high centre mid.
  2. High ball down our left, into their right-back area.
  3. The left winger sprints onto it.
  4. The striker and the high centre mid follow up, and the right winger comes infield to support.
  5. The deep centre mid moves up to the centre circle, and the back three push up.
  1. The 9 taps it back to the 10.
  2. High ball down our left, into their right-back area.
  3. The left wing back sprints onto it.
  4. The 9 and the 10 follow up, and the right wing back comes infield to support. One centre mid runs forward.
  5. The other centre mid moves to the centre circle, and the centre backs push up.
If it breaks down: drop back into shape.

Out of possession

Their kick-off

Watch our press on their kick-off
Gold: their kick-off, back to a centre mid. Blue: our press.

Hold our shape, then press on a trigger: when they play it back, a bad touch, or a player receiving facing her own goal. These are the same triggers as their goal kicks.

Who does what

  • Striker: hunts the ball.
  • Wingers: step inside to close the passing options through the middle.
  • Centre mids: step up behind the press.
  • Wide centre backs: step out to pick up their wingers.
  • The 9 and the 10: hunt the ball.
  • Wing backs: step in front of their wingers, and follow them if the ball goes wide.
  • Centre mids: form the spine of the team.
  • Centre backs: step up a little.
